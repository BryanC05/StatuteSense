## 📜 Statutory & Case Law Citation Engine

### 1. 100% Verified Legal Precedents Bank ([lib/ai.js](file:///Users/user/OpenCode/StatuteSense/lib/ai.js))
- Strict system prompt rules eliminate AI hallucinations and fake reporter volume/page numbers.
- Grounded in verified statutes and landmark case precedents:
  - **Defend Trade Secrets Act (DTSA), 18 U.S.C. § 1836 et seq.** / **UTSA § 1(4)**
  - **Restatement (Second) of Contracts § 71 & § 75** (Exchange & Consideration)
  - **E.I. du Pont de Nemours & Co. v. Christopher, 431 F.2d 1012 (5th Cir. 1970)**
  - **Rockwell Graphic Systems, Inc. v. DEV Industries, Inc., 925 F.2d 174 (7th Cir. 1991)**
  - **Silvaco Data Systems v. Intel Corp., 184 Cal. App. 4th 210 (2010)**

### 2. Contextual Scope Rules & RAG JSON Legal Schema ([app/api/analyze/route.js](file:///Users/user/OpenCode/StatuteSense/app/api/analyze/route.js))
- **UCC Article 2 Rule**: Only cites UCC Article 2 when tangible goods or products are involved.
- **GDPR / CCPA / DPA Rule**: Only flags privacy statutes if PII or consumer records are present.
- **JSON Legal Schema Output**: Appends structured JSON Legal Schema (`document_type`, `meta`, `clauses`, `legal_citations`) at the end of outputs for RAG vector stores.
